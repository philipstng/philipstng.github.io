Prompt Forge

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version

  • Prompt Forge keeps your projects, sparks, and settings on your own device. We do not run accounts, and we do not operate servers that store your prompts.
  • Your prompts never leave your device unless you choose to export them, copy them out of the app, or share an exported file yourself.
  • The Android version of the app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to show and measure those ads. This is explained in section 5, along with how to limit it.
  • The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your device contacts Google when the app starts.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use tracking for our own analytics.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Prompt Forge (the "App", "we", "us") handles, why, where it is stored, who else may receive it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Prompt Forge web app and to the Android application that wraps it (together, the "Services").

This policy does not cover third-party websites or services that we link to, or the way Google handles information outside of the App. Please read those providers' policies directly.

If you do not agree with this policy, please stop using the Services and uninstall the Android app or close the web page. Uninstalling or clearing the App's data removes the information described in section 3.

3. Information stored on your device

Prompt Forge is designed to work offline. The information you create is saved in your browser's or the Android WebView's local storage on your device. Nothing in this group is uploaded to our servers.

What is storedWhere it livesWhy
Projects: name, 3-letter project code (for example LAX), chosen emoji, creation date Local storage key promptForge.v1 To show your projects and keep their codes unchanged
Sparks: prompt text, Freestyle or Template fields (Where, Action, What, Limits, Format), optional note, applied status, timestamps, spark number Local storage key promptForge.v1 To store and show your prompts, group them by day, and build copy-ready prompt text
Appearance settings: light or dark mode and the chosen colour preset Local storage key promptForge.theme and the settings record in promptForge.v1 To remember how you like the App to look
Guide status: whether you have seen the Quick Guide Local storage key hasCompletedTutorial So the guide opens automatically only on your first visit

Local storage is a standard browser feature. It is separate for each app or website, it does not expire on its own, and it is not sent to us. It is removed when you clear the App's data, clear your browser's site data, or uninstall the Android app.

Prompt Forge does not use cookies of its own.

Token-savings estimates

The App calculates an estimated token-savings percentage and signal bars from the number of prompts in a spark. These calculations happen entirely on your device. They are estimates only and are not sent anywhere.

4. Information you export or import

The Settings screen lets you export your library as a JSON file (named prompt-forge-vault-YYYY-MM-DD.json) and import a file you saved earlier.

  • Export creates the file on your device and saves it wherever your device saves downloads. We do not receive a copy.
  • Import reads the file you choose on your device and replaces the current library after you confirm. The file is not uploaded.
  • Exported files contain everything in your library, including any prompt text you have written, and any personal information you typed into prompts. Treat exported files as private. Anyone who opens the file can read its contents.

5. Advertising in the Android app

The Android version of Prompt Forge displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC and its affiliates, the "Google Mobile Ads SDK"). The web version does not show these advertisements.

What Google may collect

To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (on Android, the Advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and browser details such as model, operating system version, screen size, language, and app version;
  • Information about the ads you see and interact with, the time and frequency of ad requests, and crash or diagnostic data;
  • Information about your interests and activity across other apps and sites, where Google's ad personalisation is enabled.

Google acts as an independent controller for the information it collects through the SDK. The content of your prompts, projects, and sparks is not sent to Google by the App as part of ad requests. Ads are chosen based on the device and request information described above.

Limiting personalised ads

  • Android: open Settings, go to Privacy (or Security and privacy), then Ads, and turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or delete the Advertising ID. You can also review Google's settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • Consent: where the law in your location requires consent for personalised advertising (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the App is intended to show a consent message before personalised ads are requested. [Confirm that the consent message (for example Google's User Messaging Platform) is live in the release build before publishing.] You can change your choice at any time through the consent settings that the message provides.
  • Non-personalised ads still show, but they are based on the context of the request rather than your personal profile.

Google's own policies

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy, and information about the SDK is available in AdMob's support pages. We do not control Google's practices, so please review those documents for details.

6. Google Fonts

The web and Android versions load typefaces (Playfair Display, Inter, Barlow Condensed, and Pinyon Script) from Google Fonts. When the App starts, your device requests those font files from Google's servers. Google may receive your IP address and the browser or device details included in that request. Google's use of this information is described in its Privacy Policy linked above.

7. How we use information

We use information in the following limited ways:

  • To operate the App's features on your device: saving your projects and sparks, numbering sparks, building copy-ready prompt text, applying your appearance settings, and showing the Quick Guide;
  • To let you copy prompt text to your clipboard, when you choose to copy;
  • To show advertisements in the Android app and to let Google measure them (see section 5);
  • To respond to privacy questions you send us, and to meet legal obligations if they arise.

We do not use your prompts or projects to train machine-learning models, build profiles about you, or sell or rent your information.

8. Clipboard use

The Copy and Copy All buttons place prompt text on your device's clipboard. Other apps can read the clipboard once you paste, and some operating systems may keep a clipboard history. Prompt Forge does not read the clipboard in the background.

9. Android permissions and device features

The Android app asks for only the permissions and device capabilities it needs to run and show ads. These are declared in the app's Android manifest and may include:

  • Internet access, so the App can load fonts and request advertisements;
  • Network state, so the App can check whether a connection is available for ad requests;
  • Advertising ID, which Google uses to deliver and measure ads, subject to your device settings.

Prompt Forge does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files. Importing and exporting vault files use the standard system file picker and save dialog, which only give the App access to the file you select.

The current Android build may use Google's test advertising unit ID during development. A published build must use your own AdMob ad unit ID and this policy should be reviewed at that point.

10. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in these situations:

  • Google: through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Fonts, as described in sections 5 and 6;
  • Legal reasons: if we are required by law, a court order, or a valid government request to disclose information, we will do so only to the extent required;
  • Business transfer: if the App is sold, merged, or transferred, this policy will continue to apply to information held by the new owner, and you will be told of any material change;
  • With your direction: when you export a vault file, copy text, or share a file or prompt through another app, the information goes where you send it.

11. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, you control how long it exists:

  • Delete a project: use the trash icon on the project page. This removes the project and all of its sparks from local storage.
  • Delete a spark: use the trash icon on the spark row.
  • Clear everything: uninstall the Android app, or clear the App's storage in your device or browser settings. This removes the local storage described in section 3.
  • Exported files: these stay wherever you saved them. Delete them directly from your device.
  • Google: information held by Google is governed by Google's retention practices. You can request deletion of information Google holds about you through Google's account and privacy tools.

We do not keep copies of your prompts, projects, or sparks, so we cannot restore data you have deleted from your device.

12. Security

Your information is protected by your device's own security features, including its lock screen, app sandboxing, and storage encryption where the operating system provides it. We do not add an extra encryption layer to the local storage. Do not store secrets, passwords, or private keys in prompts. Use a lock screen on your device, keep your operating system updated, and store exported vault files in a secure location.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information held on a device we do not control.
